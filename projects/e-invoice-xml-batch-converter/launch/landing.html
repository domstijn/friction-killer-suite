FAQ

Are my confidential B2B XML invoices uploaded anywhere?

Never. Browser DOMParser reads your XML files 100% locally in memory—you can even test offline once the page is open, or use our Python .whl CLI in air-gapped accounting environments.

Which formats are supported?

Both EU EN 16931 syntaxes: OASIS UBL 2.1 (XRechnung & Belgian/EU Peppol BIS Billing 3.0) and UN/CEFACT CII (ZUGFeRD 2.x / Factur-X XML).

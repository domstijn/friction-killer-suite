FAQ

Do I need to connect my Amazon Seller Central SP-API account?

No. Download your Settlement Flat-File V2 (.txt or .tsv) from Seller Central → Reports → Payments and drop it right into the browser app. Zero API tokens leave your computer.

How does it handle Account-Level Reserves?

Both Previous Reserve Amount Balance (credit release) and Current Reserve Amount (debit holdback) are netted into the clearing Reserve Hold/Release account (Xero 125 / SKR03 1365) so Debits always equal Credits against your exact bank deposit.
